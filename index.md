Hello, my name is Ramona Kühn and I am a researcher in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP). I have a Master in Computer Science from the University of Passau (focus area: Security and Reliability). My current research focuses on the computational processing of rhetorical figures, especially in German. Tasks encompass building ontologies, detection algorithms, and developing ways for data annotation.
On this website, you can find my publications, resources, and information about myself.


## Current Position
I am currently a PhD student at the Chair of Data Science and the [CAROLL Research Group](https://ca-roll.github.io/) - Computational Rhetoric in Social Media and Law at the University of Passau.
