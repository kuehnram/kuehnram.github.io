# Ramona Kühn

I work as a software quality consultant for [Teamscale by CQSE](https://teamscale.com/). I help developers and testers to find more bugs in less time and speed up their test suite with the help of AI.
Furtermore, I am a researcher in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP). I have a Master in Computer Science from the University of Passau (focus area: Security and Reliability). In my PhD thesis, my research focuses on the computational processing of rhetorical figures, especially in German. Tasks encompass building ontologies, detection algorithms, and developing ways for data annotation.
On this website, you can find my publications, talks, resources, and information about myself.


## Current Position
I am currently working as a software quality consultant at CQSE, where I help customers set up [Teamscale](https://teamscale.com/), the software intelligence platform. Previously, I was a PhD student at the Chair of Data Science and the [CAROLL Research Group](https://ca-roll.github.io/) - Computational Rhetoric in Social Media and Law at the University of Passau.
